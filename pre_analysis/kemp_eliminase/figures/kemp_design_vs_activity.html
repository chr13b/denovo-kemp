Kemp eliminase designs vs catalytic activity

Published kcat/KM for each design family set against its catalytic residues and the geometry seen in crystal structures. Hover any mark for the value, source and caveats; every chart has a table view.

Where designs start

Every design began at 12–210 M⁻¹s⁻¹ (1A53-2: 64), whatever its base. Optimised variants then span 2,600 to 230,000.

Computational only is close

With no library screening, Des27.7 (12,700), KempTIM after ensemble redesign (32,000) and Des27.7 plus one engineered residue (>100,000) are within roughly 2–18× of HG3.17 (230,000).

Static geometry does not separate them

Base to ring N is 2.25–2.72 Å from 125 to 230,000 M⁻¹s⁻¹. Des27 and Des27.7 differ by about 100× in activity yet match to 0.01 Å (2.72 vs 2.71 Å) and 0.04 Å (3.96 vs 3.92 Å).

Tight stacking is not required

IGPS designs sit 3.6–4.0 Å from an aromatic ring; HG3 is 5.1 Å and HG3.17 6.3 Å, the most active enzyme here. (HG3.17 value unverified in the paper text.)

Read with care

  • Conditions (pH, temperature, substrate, buffer) differ between papers; compare orders of magnitude, not small differences.
  • HG3.17 = 230,000 is from a web summary of Blomberg 2013, not the paper text. "≤" values are my calculation from a stated fold-change (125 × 250, 57 × 250, 32,000 ÷ 1,600), not reported numbers.
  • KE59 uses 5-nitrobenzisoxazole (60,430); with 5,7-dichlorobenzisoxazole the same variant reaches 573,090 and is not comparable.
  • Geometry is from TS-analog complexes (6NT, 3NY, 0CU, 0CT) or modelled substrate poses, not the true transition state. About ten structures with a matching kinetic value: this shows a lack of correlation, not proof of its absence.
  • Source files: figures/data/data.json, designs.csv, geometry.csv; standalone charts in figures/svg/.
