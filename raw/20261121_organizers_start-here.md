# Start here

Three things, in this order.

| | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Connect to your cluster | `CONNECT.txt` in your team packet |
| 2 | Learn the workflow and run the example | `GUIDE.html` |
| 3 | Submit your designs | `SUBMISSION.md` |

Your team packet holds your private key and your cluster's address. It is yours
alone — do not share it with another team.

**Two things worth knowing before you start.**

Your team can run **two GPUs at once** (two `g6.xlarge`), or one larger node
(`g6.2xlarge`) when you need the extra host memory for building an MSA. Not both.
`GUIDE.html` explains which to use when.

Your design has to be **novel**: a backbone with TM-score > 0.7 to a published
structure, or a sequence with more than 70% identity to a published enzyme, does
not qualify. `SUBMISSION.md` has the detail.

Ask an organiser whenever something is unclear. That is faster than guessing.
