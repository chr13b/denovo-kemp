An independent scorer agrees

Zonghu’s weighted K1–K5, his own implementation with a different target structure, ranks the same designs. On single folds his score and our D correlate at Spearman −0.97 over 44 shared structures. Both analyses found independently that a single fold overstates a design.

A weakness we share

The angle measured at the heteroatom lies outside the crystal range in every one of the 48 replicated designs — median 40° outside. Under our own angle definition the same designs are close to the window. The organizers do not say which definition they use.

s010 leads on both of our geometry scores, and it is a close variant rather than the parent. s024 is the only design that satisfies the pre-registered rule, but it has one seed in five that loses the ligand entirely. The honest part is on the right: under one plausible definition of the approach angle, every single one of our forty-eight replicated designs sits outside the crystal range. If the organizers use that definition, nothing we generated was selected for it.
