Method

The pipeline, stage by stage

Motif

HG3.17 Asp and Gln plus 6NT. Asp held by OD1, CG, OD2; Gln by OE1, CD, NE2; the whole ligand rigid.

RFD3

A 260-residue backbone, no side chains. 12 min.

LigandMPNN

Sequences at T 0.1/0.2/0.3, the two catalytic residues never redesigned. 6 s per batch.

Boltz-2

200 sampling steps, 3 recycling, 1 diffusion sample. 19 s per fold.

Measure

Declared residues, with a nearest-atom scan as a cross-check.

One backbone, chosen on designability

Backbones were compared on the median CA RMSD between the designed backbone and its refold. It separates backbones but says nothing about an individual sequence. Every design sits on seed 4, base at position 86, donor at 52.

What we skip, and why

The MSA stage is a no-op for de novo sequences — no homologue exists, depth 1, and the output is bit-identical with and without it. Skipping MMseqs2 gives a 3–4× gain.

Motif in, backbone out, sequences onto that backbone, then the same cofold the organizers use. Two decisions worth naming. Every design sits on one backbone, seed four, chosen on designability — how closely its refold reproduces the designed backbone. And we skip the MSA stage entirely: for a de novo sequence there is no homologue, the output is bit-identical, and dropping it makes the whole loop three to four times faster.
