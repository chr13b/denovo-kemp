Biasing LigandMPNN: three arms

A bias is added to the logit before the division by temperature, so at T = 0.3 a bias of 1.5 is a log-odds shift of 5 — about 148 in odds. Small numbers, decisive effect. The two catalytic residues are held by a separate mechanism and were intact in all 216 sequences.

Close variants work

An 11-fold enrichment of the good tail, dose-dependent within the arm. But no single mutation is established: 133 of 260 positions vary, and the strongest candidate is absent from the two best replicated designs.

Polar-down does not

A real null, not a failed bias: it cut the polar count by 58 % as intended. At the replicated level the enrichment is weaker than at single-fold level.

LigandMPNN samples each position from a softmax, and a bias file shifts those logits before the temperature division — which is why a bias of one point five is decisive at temperature zero point three. Three arms. Staying close to a known-good parent enriches the good tail elevenfold. Pushing polar residues out of the pocket, which we read off the crystals, does nothing — and we know the bias worked, because the polar count fell by more than half. So that is a real null.
