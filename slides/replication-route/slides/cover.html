The Triad · Team 01 · Perlin × D-BSSE 2026

From motif to replicated designs

Generate many sequences on one good backbone, screen cheaply on one fold, replicate the best over five seeds, and select on the replicated numbers and a novelty gate.

We design 260-residue proteins around the two catalytic contacts of a Kemp eliminase — an Asp base and a Gln donor, held around the transition-state analogue 6NT. Backbones from RFdiffusion3, sequences from LigandMPNN, and every sequence judged by the same cofold the organizers use. The central difficulty, and the reason for the whole method, is on slide six.
