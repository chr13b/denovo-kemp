A single cofold is so noisy that ranking designs by one fold ranks noise.

So the method follows from the problem: generate many sequences on one good backbone, screen cheaply on one fold, replicate the best over five seeds, and select on the replicated numbers.

This is the finding the whole method is built around. RFdiffusion reproduces the crystal contact window essentially exactly, within about a hundredth of an ångström, on every run. So the geometry is not lost at the drawing board — it is lost in the refold, which is the thing that gets scored. And a single refold is so noisy, up to an ångström on a contact, that ranking designs by one fold is ranking noise. Hence: screen cheap, then replicate.
