The brief

What is scored, and what we measure instead

The organizers cofold each submitted sequence with 6NT in Boltz-2, five replicates, and score five terms: contact distances, approach geometry, interface confidence, active-site packing and pose consistency.

Geometry — distances and approach together — outweighs everything else combined, and the terms multiply. Novelty, backbone TM-score at most 0.7 and sequence identity at most 70 %, is a gate rather than a score.

We do not know the functional forms

So we measure the quantities ourselves and build proxies, against windows taken from the crystal structures.
