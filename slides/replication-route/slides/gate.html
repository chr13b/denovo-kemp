F3, written before the data

Both distances inside the crystal window in at least 3 of 5 seeds, mean D over the five seeds at most 15, and OD2 the nearest oxygen in at least 4 of 5. Only s024 satisfies it.

F4, novelty

qTM at most 0.70 against the Kemp family and 40,066 PDB representatives. Our top designs: family qTM 0.29–0.30, PDB qTM 0.55–0.57, and MMseqs2 found no homologue at all.

The filters run in a fixed order and most of the work is done early: eighty-four per cent of designs simply do not place the ligand. The free confidence filter removes another eighty-three per cent while keeping five of the six best designs. Then five-seed replication, then the rule we wrote before seeing the replication data. Only s024 satisfies it. Several close variants have better replicated geometry but miss the in-window count, and adding them would be an amendment made after seeing the data.
