Design choices

Where we corrected ourselves

First assumption What the data showed What we did
The crystal Trp is a pi stack on the ligand In 7K4Z it is an offset edge and nitro contact, with 0 % ring overlap Kept it as B; added B2 as a true parallel-displaced stack
The stacking residue can be left to the generator Des27 and KE70 both rely on an aromatic stacking residue Put the Trp into the theozyme
The base meets either lone pair It meets the anti lone pair, dihedral 157–178° Base position taken from the crystal, not re-derived
An Asp + Ser donor variant is worth testing Ser, His and Lys at position 50 leave a water in the donor site Dropped it; kept Gln as the donor
Pilot designs are too short to bury the ligand Burial does not track chain length (r ≈ 0.12); it tracks contact residues (0.58) and ligand SASA (−0.62) Retracted the claim; added burial conditioning and a gate
Pilot design B_16 looks strong Base O to N3 was 3.87 Å and ligand spread 1.96 Å once scores were read Excluded it; B_18 was added instead
Six early assumptions turned out wrong or incomplete, and each correction changed what we built. The most consequential is the last pair: we thought our pilot designs were too short to bury the ligand, but burial does not track chain length at all — it tracks contact residues and ligand exposure. So we retracted that and added conditioning instead of making proteins longer.
