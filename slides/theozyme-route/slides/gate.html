The pilot

Five variants, two survivors

A 100-design pilot — 20 per variant, about $1.3 in total — showed that the donor matters and that B and B2 are the two variants worth scaling. Boltz-2 scored 68 of the 100.

A and C dropped

A was clearly worst and C was held back by the missing donor. B led B2 at moderate confidence. A fifth variant, B_soft, was generated but never scored.

The stack behaved as intended

B2 gave 20 of 20 true face stacks; B gave 16 offset and 4 face.

Burial was the weak term

Median CA count within 12 Å was 24–34 in the pilot against 46–47 in the crystals — which led to the burial conditioning and gate.

A hundred designs, twenty per variant, for about one dollar thirty. Two things came out of it. The donor matters: variant C, which drops the Gln, is clearly held back. And burial was our weak term — the pilot sat at twenty-four to thirty-four CA atoms against forty-six to forty-seven in the crystals, which is what led to the burial conditioning in production.
