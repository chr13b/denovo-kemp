The brief

What is scored, and the limits

iPDE

Boltz-2 ligand placement.

Geometry

Base and hydrogen-bond donor.

Burial

CA atoms within 12 Å of the catalytic base.

Consistency

Agreement over 5 Boltz-2 samples.

Size and novelty

At most 260 residues. At most 70 % sequence identity to published enzymes, and TM-score at most 0.7 to published structures.

Lab validation

At least 5 designs go to the lab.

Budget

$30 for the whole campaign, plus a self-set 5 CHF overage cap.

Four scored terms: how confidently Boltz-2 places the ligand, the geometry of the base and the donor, how buried the catalytic base is — counted as CA atoms within twelve ångström — and how consistent all of that is across five Boltz-2 samples. The hard limits are 260 residues, 70 per cent identity and TM-score 0.7. And a thirty dollar budget for the entire campaign.
