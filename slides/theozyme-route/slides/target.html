The theozyme

Why this theozyme

We fixed the fewest residues that carry the chemistry — Asp base, Gln donor, Trp contact, plus the ligand — and took every coordinate from crystal structures with the same ligand, not from an idealised model.

Literature anchor

Asp base with a Gln oxyanion donor is the arrangement in HG3.17 (Asp127, Gln50). Des27 uses an Asp162 base and a Phe113 stack with no donor, and loses activity when the Asp is changed to Ala. KE70 uses a His–Asp dyad with a Tyr48 stack.

Measured, not assumed

9 copies of 6NT across five crystal structures — 7K4Z, 7K4Q, 6SRY, 6SRZ, 6SS3. The ligand pose agrees to 0.05–0.12 Å. Base Asp O to N3(H) is 2.40–2.65 Å with N–H···O of 162–170°.

Burial benchmark, and environment through conditioning

In the crystals 46–47 CA atoms lie within 12 Å of the Asp CG, and no Lys or Arg is within 10 Å of the carboxylate. Rather than fixing further residues, we let RFD4 shape the pocket through burial and hydrogen-bond conditions.

Two principles. Fix as little as possible — only the residues that do the chemistry — and take every coordinate from a crystal structure with the same ligand rather than from an idealised model. We measured nine copies of the ligand across five structures and they agree to about a tenth of an ångström. The burial number, forty-six to forty-seven CA atoms within twelve ångström, became our benchmark. Everything else about the pocket we left to conditioning rather than fixing more residues.
