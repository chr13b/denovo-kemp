The two theozymes: B and B2

Both hold the same three catalytic residues plus the ligand, and differ only in where the Trp sits.

One theozyme, two Trp geometries. Everything else is identical — the same Asp base, the same Gln donor, the same ligand, twenty-seven fixed atoms in total. The only difference is where the tryptophan sits: B keeps the crystal's offset edge contact, B2 uses a textbook parallel-displaced stack. Since the Trp was the part we were least sure about, we made it the experiment rather than guessing.
