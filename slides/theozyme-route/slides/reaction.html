The brief

The task and how it is scored

A de novo Kemp eliminase for 5-nitrobenzisoxazole, with 6-nitrobenzotriazole (6NT) as the transition-state analogue ligand, and one theozyme per team.

Designs are generated with RFD4-Proteina and scored with Boltz-2.

Two contacts carry the chemistry

Asp OD2 to ligand N3, and Gln NE2 to ligand N1. Both distances are taken from the 7K4Z crystal structure.

Asp OD2 → N3

2.51 Å, the catalytic base.

Gln NE2 → N1

3.10 Å, the hydrogen-bond donor.

The brief is a de novo Kemp eliminase for 5-nitrobenzisoxazole. We never see the substrate itself: the ligand in every scored structure is 6NT, a transition-state analogue. Each team supplies one theozyme. Generation is RFD4-Proteina, scoring is Boltz-2. The two distances on the right are the whole active site, both taken from the 7K4Z crystal structure.
