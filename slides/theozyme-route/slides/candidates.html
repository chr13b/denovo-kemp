What the strict test says

Only JJ1, JJ3 and JJ7 reproduce the designed base geometry in 4 of 5 samples. Donor geometry is fixed by the motif but not measured per sample.

The gates, not the scores, did most of the selecting: of two and a half thousand scored designs only fifty-nine placed the ligand correctly. We fixed the rules in code before looking at which designs would pass. Ranking breaks ties in order — iPDE, then ligand spread, then burial, then ligand exposure, then cysteine count. Pick rules required at least two designs per theozyme family, different lengths, and no two picks above eighty per cent identity to each other. Two were added by hand and are flagged. The honest caveat is the last column: only three of the ten reproduce the designed base geometry in four of five samples.
