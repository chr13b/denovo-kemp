  • Two residues do the chemistry: a base and a donor
  • Generate with RFD4-Proteina or RFdiffusion3

Base → N3

Asp or Glu. 2.51 Å.

Donor → N1

Gln and others. 3.10 Å.

The brief is a de novo Kemp eliminase for 5-nitrobenzisoxazole. We never see the substrate: every scored structure contains 6NT, a stable stand-in for the transition state. Two residues do all the chemistry. A base takes the proton off the ring, the ring opens, and a donor catches the negative charge. Both target distances come from crystal structures.
