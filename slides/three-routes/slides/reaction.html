The task

  • De novo Kemp eliminase for 5-nitrobenzisoxazole
  • A base takes the proton, the ring opens
  • A donor catches the charge on the leaving oxygen
  • Scored against 6NT, the transition-state analogue
  • Judged by a Boltz-2 cofold, 5 replicates

Reaction scheme reproduced from Blomberg et al., Nature 503, 418–421 (2013).

The brief is a de novo Kemp eliminase for 5-nitrobenzisoxazole. A base takes the proton off C3, the isoxazole ring opens, and a donor stabilises the negative charge that appears on the leaving oxygen. We never see that substrate: every scored structure contains 6NT, structure three in the scheme, a stable stand-in for the transition state. Its two ring nitrogens are where the base and the donor have to sit.
