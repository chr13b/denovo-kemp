An optimum, not a direction

Hard limits

≤ 260 residues · TM ≤ 0.7 · identity ≤ 70 % · 20 designs

Four scored terms, and geometry dominates. Three rules shaped every decision. The terms multiply, so a brilliant confidence score cannot rescue a broken contact. Each distance has an optimum rather than a direction, so closer is not better. And novelty is a hard gate, not a bonus. That last one is the real tension: we have to be close to a working active site and far from any published protein.
