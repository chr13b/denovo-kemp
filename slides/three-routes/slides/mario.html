designs generated over four campaigns. Six hold up under fresh seeds.

Route three is combinatorial. Instead of copying one crystal site, it mines a hundred published entries for the contacts that actually occur, then assembles a theozyme from four of them, each from a different structure. That includes a backbone N–H to the nitro group, which neither other route uses. It also puts a cheap filter in the middle: an OpenFold3 refold on everything, seconds per design, deciding what is worth a Boltz run.
