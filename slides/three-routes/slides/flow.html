The same three approaches as a flow. Each produces designs its own way, and from there the path is shared: one scoring step on geometry, burial, a valid declaration and whether the catalytic residues sit in a loop, then the Boltz-2 cofold that the organizers themselves use. The dashed line is the loop that matters: every cofold goes back through the scorer, so nothing is judged on the structure it was designed as. Only after that does the novelty gate decide which designs can take a submission slot.
