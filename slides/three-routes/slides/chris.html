Route two is evolutionary, and it starts from a measurement. The generator gets the geometry right every time, and the refold destroys it. A single refold is too noisy to rank by. So the method is breadth plus replication: many sequences on one backbone, biased towards a parent that already worked, then five seeds each before anything is believed. The honest tension is that only one design passes the rule we wrote in advance, and four that score better do not.
