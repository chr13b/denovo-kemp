Route two is evolutionary, and it starts from a measurement. The generator gets the geometry right every time and the refold destroys it: the bars on the right are the same contact before and after. A single refold is too noisy to rank by. So the method is breadth plus replication, biased towards a parent that already worked. The honest tension is that only one design passes the rule we wrote in advance, and four that score better do not.
